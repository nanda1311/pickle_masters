The Founder's Vision

Chiranjeevi is an expert at making pickles. He makes each jar using old family recipes passed down for generations. He learned from Andhra Pradesh's food traditions, where strong spices and fresh ingredients create tasty flavors. He started this business to share that real taste with everyone who wants true homemade goodness.

Since 2020

Chiranjeevi Venkata Subbaraju

Started our pickle company to share the rich tradition of Andhra pickles with real, authentic taste.

Expertise in Authenticity

With years of hands-on experience, Chiranjeevi makes every batch carefully—balancing hot chilies, sour tamarind, and fresh vegetables. No shortcuts or chemicals; only good quality, local ingredients keep the pickles crunchy and tasty. His methods preserve the true spirit of Andhra pickles, making them a favorite with meals and snacks.

Growing Success Since 2020

Starting during tough times, the business has grown through loyal customers who love the great taste. Today, we deliver across the country, spreading Chiranjeevi's dream of bringing real Andhra taste to every home. Join thousands enjoying our tradition in every spicy, tasty bite.
