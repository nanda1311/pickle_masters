Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

At Pickle Masters (“we”, “us” or “our”), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit picklemasters.in or purchase our products, including how your payment information is handled by our payment gateway partner, Razorpay.

  1. Information We Collect
    • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address provided during account creation, checkout, or customer support interaction.
    • Order Information: Products purchased, order value, order status, and delivery pincode.
    • Payment Information: When you pay online, your card, UPI, net-banking, or wallet details are entered directly on Razorpay's secure payment page and are collected and processed by Razorpay, not by us. We only receive a payment status (success/failure) and a transaction/reference ID for your order.
    • Non-Personal Information: Browsing data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected via cookies.
    • Return/Refund Evidence: Photos or videos you submit for damaged, spoiled, or incorrect item claims, used only for processing that claim.
  2. How We Use Your Information
    • To process, fulfil, and deliver your orders.
    • To initiate and process online payments and refunds through Razorpay.
    • To verify and process cancellation, return, or refund requests.
    • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support communications.
    • To send offers and promotional communications, which you may opt out of at any time.
    • To comply with legal obligations and to detect or prevent fraud.
  3. How We Share Your Information
    • We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only where necessary, with:
    • Razorpay (Payment Gateway): Your name, contact details, and order amount are shared with Razorpay solely to process online payments and refunds. Razorpay processes this data under its own privacy policy and is PCI-DSS compliant. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or banking passwords.
    • Logistics/Courier Partners: Your name, phone number, and delivery address, to enable order delivery.
    • Legal Authorities: Where required to comply with applicable law or to protect our legal rights.
    • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
    • We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and analyze site traffic.
    • You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect site functionality such as checkout.
  5. Payment Security
    • All online payments are processed through Razorpay over an encrypted (SSL/TLS) connection on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
    • We do not store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials on our servers at any time.
  6. Data Security
    • We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including SSL encryption and secure hosting, to protect your personal information.
    • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
  7. Your Rights
    • Subject to applicable law, you may:
    • Access, update, or correct your personal data via your profile page.
    • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
    • Opt out of promotional emails, SMS, or calls at any time.
    • For any privacy-related request or grievance, contact us at info@picklemasters.com.
  8. Data Retention
    • We retain your personal and order information for as long as your account is active or as needed to fulfil orders, resolve disputes, and comply with legal, tax, and accounting requirements.
  9. Third-Party Links
    • Our Website may contain links to third-party sites, including Razorpay's payment page. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies; please review them before sharing your data.
  10. Children's Privacy
    • Our Website is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent.
  11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
    • We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
