Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

At Pickle Masters, we take care to pack every order carefully. Because our products are perishable, consumable food items, we are unable to accept returns once a product has been delivered, opened, or consumed, except in the specific cases described below.

  1. Eligibility for Return or Refund
    • Damaged in Transit: The jar/packaging arrived broken, leaking, or damaged.
    • Spoiled or Quality Issue: The product shows visible signs of spoilage or quality defect on delivery.
    • Wrong or Missing Item: You received a different product than ordered, or an item from your order is missing.

    We do not accept returns for reasons of personal taste preference, or once a jar has been opened and part of the product consumed, unless one of the above issues applies.

  2. Conditions for Raising a Claim
    Claim must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
    Clear photos (and an unboxing video for damaged/leaking packaging, if available) must be shared as proof.
    The Order ID must be provided with the claim.
  3. How to Request a Return or Refund
    1. Email info@picklemasters.com or call +91 9441373245 within 48 hours of delivery.
    2. Share your Order ID, a description of the issue, and photo/video evidence.
    3. Our team will review your claim and respond within 2-3 business days.
    4. If approved, we will offer a replacement (subject to stock availability) or a refund, as you prefer.
  4. Refund Timelines
    • Approved refunds for orders paid online via Razorpay are credited to your original payment method (card/UPI/net banking/wallet) within 5-7 business days of approval.
    • Approved refunds for Cash on Delivery orders are processed via bank transfer or UPI to the details you provide, within 5-7 business days of approval.
    • Shipping charges, where applicable, are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (damaged, wrong, or missing item).
  5. Contact Us
    • Email: info@picklemasters.com
    • Phone: +91 9441373245
    • Address: Pickle Masters, L.No.140, Street-A11, Nalla Krishnaveni Layout, Tanuku, West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh - 534211

By placing an order with us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Return and Refund Policy.
