Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2025

At Bata Stores (“we”, “us” or “our”), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our website or interact with our services.

    • Non-Personal Information: Browsing data such as IP address, browser type, device info, and pages visited (via cookies).
    • Unboxing Videos: Required for return claims; used only for processing returns.
  2. How We Use Your Information
    • To process and fulfill orders, including shipping and payments.

    • To communicate offers, unless you request not to.
    • To comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.
  3. How We Share Your Information
    • We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data with:

    • Business Transfers: In case of acquisition or restructuring, your data may be transferred.
  4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
    • We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyze site traffic.
    • You can customize cookie access via browser settings. Disabling may affect functionality.
  5. Data Security
    • We use security protocols like SSL encryption and secure hosting.
    • However, no method is fully secure; we cannot guarantee absolute safety.
  6. Your Rights
    • Depending on local law, you may have the right to:
    • Access, update, or delete your personal data.
    • Opt out of certain promotional communications.
    • Contact us to process your information.

  7. Data Retention
    • We retain your information only as long as needed for our purposes or as required by law.

  9. Children’s Privacy
