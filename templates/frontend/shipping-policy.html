Shipping Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how we process, pack, and deliver your Pickle Masters order.

  1. Serviceable Areas
    • We currently deliver to pincodes listed as serviceable on our Website. You can check whether we deliver to your location by entering your pincode on the product or checkout page before placing an order.
    • If your pincode is not serviceable, you will not be able to complete checkout for delivery to that address.
  2. Order Processing
    • Orders are processed and packed within 1-2 business days of order confirmation (i.e., after payment is confirmed for online orders, or immediately for Cash on Delivery orders).
    • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next business day.
  3. Delivery Timelines
    • Once dispatched, orders are typically delivered within 4-7 business days, depending on your delivery location.
    • Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by courier delays, weather, regional restrictions, or other circumstances beyond our control.
  4. Shipping Charges
    • Applicable shipping charges, if any, are calculated based on your delivery location and order value, and are shown at checkout before you confirm your order.
  5. Order Tracking
    • Once your order is dispatched, you can view its status under "My Orders" in your account. Courier tracking details, where available, will be shared with you by email or SMS.
  6. Delivery Issues
    • Please inspect your package at the time of delivery. If it arrives damaged, spoiled, or with missing/incorrect items, report it to us within 48 hours as described in our Return and Refund Policy.
    • If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address, unavailability of the recipient, or refusal to accept COD payment, we may charge reasonable re-delivery or return-shipping costs before attempting redelivery.
  7. Contact Us
    • Email: info@picklemasters.com
    • Phone: +91 9441373245
    • Address: Pickle Masters, L.No.140, Street-A11, Nalla Krishnaveni Layout, Tanuku, West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh - 534211
