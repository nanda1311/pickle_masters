Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

At Pickle Masters, our products are freshly prepared and packed for dispatch soon after your order is placed. This Cancellation Policy explains when and how you may cancel an order.

  1. Cancellation Window
    • You may cancel your order free of charge within 24 hours of placing it, or any time before it has been dispatched for delivery — whichever is earlier.
    • Once an order has been dispatched/handed over to our courier partner, it can no longer be cancelled. You may still be eligible for a return or refund under our Return and Refund Policy if the item arrives damaged, spoiled, or incorrect.
  2. How to Cancel an Order
    • Email us at info@picklemasters.com or call +91 9441373245 with your Order ID and reason for cancellation.
    • Our support team will confirm the cancellation status by email or phone within 24 hours of your request.
  3. Order Modifications
    • Changes to delivery address, quantity, or items can only be accommodated before the order is dispatched. Contact us as soon as possible to request a change.
  4. Refunds on Cancellation
    • For orders paid online via Razorpay (card/UPI/net banking/wallet), the full amount is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds are initiated within 2 business days of the cancellation being confirmed and typically reflect in your account within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
    • For Cash on Delivery orders, no payment is collected upon cancellation before dispatch.
    • If an order is cancelled by us (for example, due to non-serviceability of your pincode or stock unavailability) after online payment has been made, the full amount will be refunded automatically to your original payment method within the same timeline.
  5. Contact Us
    • Email: info@picklemasters.com
    • Phone: +91 9441373245
    • Address: Pickle Masters, L.No.140, Street-A11, Nalla Krishnaveni Layout, Tanuku, West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh - 534211

By placing an order with us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Cancellation Policy.
