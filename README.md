# Pickle Masters

Pickle Masters is an online store for authentic South Indian pickles, established in 2020. The platform lets customers browse vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and Gongura combination pickles, and order them for home delivery, all made from fresh, high-quality ingredients following traditional recipes.

---

## Overview

Pickle Masters operates as a focused e-commerce storefront built around a single product category: handmade pickles. The site is organized around three main product lines, supported by account management, customer reviews, and standard e-commerce policy pages.

The platform serves one primary type of user:

- **Customers**, who browse pickle categories, manage a cart, create an account, and place orders for delivery.

An underlying admin/catalog layer (not customer-facing) maintains product listings, pricing, categories, and stock.

---

## How the Platform Works

### 1. Browsing and Discovery

1. A visitor lands on the homepage, which showcases the three product categories directly: Veg Pickles, Non-Veg Pickles, and Gongura Combination Pickles, each shown as a horizontally scrollable carousel of featured products.
2. From the top navigation, the visitor can go directly to a full category page (Veg, Non Veg, or Gongura) to see the complete product range, or use the "View More" link at the end of each homepage carousel.
3. Each product tile displays its name, category, the original price, and the discounted price, along with an "Add to Cart" action available directly from the listing.

### 2. Product Selection and Cart

1. Clicking a product opens its individual product page with full details and pricing.
2. From either the carousel, the category listing, or the product page, the customer can add the item directly to the cart without leaving the page.
3. The cart, accessible from the header at all times, holds selected items until the customer proceeds to checkout.

### 3. Account and Checkout

1. Customers can access their account through the Profile section in the header to manage orders and personal details.
2. From the Cart page, the customer proceeds through checkout to complete the purchase.
3. Delivery, cancellation, and refund terms are available through the footer policy links (Shipping Policy, Return and Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy) at any stage of this process.

### 4. Trust and Social Proof

1. The homepage features a rolling set of customer testimonials with star ratings, names, and time-stamped reviews, reinforcing product quality to new visitors.
2. The About Us page tells the brand's story, reinforcing the "authentic, fresh, homemade" positioning behind the products.

### 5. Support

1. Customers with questions can reach the team through the Contact Us page, by phone, or by email, with listed business hours of 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.
2. The business address and contact number are also displayed in the site footer for quick reference.

## Core Features

- Product catalog organized into three categories: Veg, Non-Veg, and Gongura Combination pickles.
- Homepage carousels highlighting featured products from each category.
- Individual product pages with pricing and discount display.
- Add-to-cart functionality available directly from listings and product pages.
- Customer account and profile management.
- Customer testimonials with star ratings displayed on the homepage.
- About Us page describing brand background and story.
- Contact Us page with phone, email, and business hours.
- Policy pages: Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping Policy, Return and Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy.
- Responsive design for both desktop and mobile browsing.
